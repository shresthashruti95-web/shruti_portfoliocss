About Me

I’m a second-year Bachelor of Information Technology (BIT) student at Model Institute of Technology, based in Kathmandu, Nepal. My background combines technology, creativity, and leadership – from exploring web and graphic design to participating in student communities, organizing workshops, and leading creative projects – and I’m now expanding that foundation into UI/UX design and front-end development. I’m passionate about creating digital experiences that are both visually engaging and meaningful.

I learn by building. Instead of waiting until I “know enough,” I turn ideas into projects and learn through the process – combining creativity, design thinking, and technology to create things that actually work. From UI/UX and web development to exploring AI and creative tools, every project is an opportunity to experiment, learn, and improve. This portfolio represents the beginning of that journey and my commitment to building strong technical and creative foundations.
